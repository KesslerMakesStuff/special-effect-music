Behind the Build

How this piece came together

I have had a deep love for Beetlejuice and the special effects and props that came with the movie. I decided that I'd like to theme part of my basement with Beetlejuice props so the first two pieces were the sign and a sandworm to compliment the sign.

I bought the 3D model from Dango88 on Cults and printed the sandworm in parts out of PLA+. I then epoxied those parts together and used a mix of sanding and filling primer to get the prop as smooth as possible.

From there I painted the body completed black. I then painted the sandworm with the signature black and white stripes. For the head within the the head, I painted with a mix of pinks and yellows for the eyes, gums, and spots. I then did light weathering around the eyes and in the white spaces to give it that distressed feel from the movie.

For the glassy eyes and wet teeth, I used a UV resin to create the glossy look.

Techniques Used

FDM printing in PLA+ and TPU Epoxy bonding Sanding Finishing Custom color mixing and testing Hand painting Weathering UV Resin
