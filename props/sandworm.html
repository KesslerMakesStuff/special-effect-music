Behind the Build

How this piece came together

I have had a deep love for Beetlejuice and the special effects and props that came with the movie. I decided I'd like to theme part of my basement with Beetlejuice props, so the first two pieces were the sign and a sandworm to complement it.

I bought the 3D model from Dango88 on Cults and printed the sandworm in parts out of PLA+. I then epoxied those parts together and used a mix of sanding and filling primer to get the prop as smooth as possible.

From there I painted the body completely black, then added the signature black and white stripes. For the inner head I used a mix of pinks and yellows for the eyes, gums, and spots, followed by light weathering to give it that distressed look from the film.

For the glassy eyes and wet teeth, I used UV resin to create the glossy finish.
