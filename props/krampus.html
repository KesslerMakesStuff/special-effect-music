Behind the Build

How this piece came together

In 2015 I saw the movie Krampus and fell in love with the creature suit and sculpt that WETA made for the character. I've always loved WETA Workshop's work, and it inspired me to try my hand at remaking the Krampus face sculpt as close as I could to the original knowing there would be differences due to sculpting on my own face cast.

For the clay I used Monster Makers medium and a mix of Xiem, Michael's, and my own handmade sculpting tools. One thing that really sped up my workflow was 3D printing custom skin texture rollers. I could get a solid base texture down quickly using impressed texturing, then iterate and refine, adding wrinkles, pores, and surface detail through additive modeling and carving.

This was a really fun sculpt to work on!
